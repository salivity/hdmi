NVIDIA G-Sync Compatible Over HDMI 2.1 VRR

NVIDIA G-Sync Compatible certification is officially supported over standard HDMI 2.1 Variable Refresh Rate (VRR) connections. While early implementations of G-Sync were strictly limited to DisplayPort, the adoption of the HDMI 2.1 standard introduced native HDMI Forum VRR, allowing compatible GeForce graphics cards to synchronize refresh rates directly with HDMI 2.1 TVs and monitors.

How NVIDIA Supports HDMI 2.1 VRR

NVIDIA integrates the HDMI Forum VRR specification directly into its graphics drivers under the "G-Sync Compatible" banner. Unlike proprietary G-Sync displays—which feature a physical hardware module that traditionally mandates a DisplayPort connection—G-Sync Compatible displays rely on open VRR standards:

  • DisplayPort connections use VESA Adaptive-Sync.
  • HDMI 2.1 connections use HDMI Forum VRR.

Because the underlying communication protocol matches the HDMI 2.1 standard, any display officially certified as "G-Sync Compatible" with HDMI 2.1 will work seamlessly over an HDMI connection. Furthermore, many non-certified HDMI 2.1 VRR displays can still run variable refresh rates manually via the NVIDIA Control Panel.

Hardware Requirements

To utilize G-Sync Compatible mode over an HDMI 2.1 connection, you must meet the following hardware and software criteria:

  • Supported GPU: GeForce RTX 30-Series (Ampere) or RTX 40-Series (Ada Lovelace) cards, which feature native fixed-rate link HDMI 2.1 output ports. Note: GeForce RTX 20-Series (Turing) cards support HDMI Forum VRR through driver-level adaptations, but their physical ports are limited to HDMI 2.0b bandwidth.
  • Cable: An officially certified Ultra High Speed HDMI cable capable of 48 Gbps bandwidth.
  • Display: A TV or monitor that supports HDMI 2.1 VRR.
  • Drivers: An updated NVIDIA Game Ready Driver (version 436.15 or newer, with current versions recommended for modern HDMI 2.1 displays).

How to Enable G-Sync Over HDMI 2.1

  1. Activate VRR on the Display: Using your monitor or TV's on-screen display (OSD), turn on Game Mode, HDMI VRR, or AMD FreeSync / G-Sync mode.
  2. Open NVIDIA Control Panel: Right-click your desktop and open the NVIDIA Control Panel.
  3. Navigate to Display Settings: Expand the Display section in the left sidebar and select Set up G-SYNC.
  4. Enable VRR: Check the box labeled Enable G-SYNC, G-SYNC Compatible and select your preferred display mode (Full screen or Windowed and full screen).
  5. Select the Display: Choose your HDMI 2.1 display from the list.
  6. Apply Specific Settings: Check Enable settings for the selected display model if the display is not automatically validated as G-Sync Compatible, then click Apply at the bottom right.
