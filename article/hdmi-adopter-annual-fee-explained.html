HDMI Adopter Annual Fee Explained

Becoming an official HDMI Adopter allows manufacturers to legally produce and sell products utilizing HDMI technology, trademarks, and intellectual property. To maintain active Adopter status with HDMI Licensing Administrator, Inc. (HDMI LA), manufacturers must pay a recurring annual licensing fee in addition to per-unit royalty charges. This guide outlines the exact annual fee structure, payment timing, and what maintaining active status entails.

The Standard Annual Fee

To maintain active status, a manufacturer must pay an annual fee of $10,000 USD.

This fee is due upon the initial execution of the HDMI Adopter Agreement and must be paid every year on the anniversary of that signing date for as long as the agreement remains in effect.

What the Annual Fee Covers

Paying the $10,000 annual fee grants a company several essential rights:

  • Access to Current Specifications: Adopters receive official access to the latest HDMI specifications, test specifications, and technical documentation.
  • Trademark and Logo Usage: Active members have the legal license to use the HDMI word mark and official logos on products, marketing collateral, and packaging.
  • HDMI Adopter Directory Listing: The company remains listed on the official HDMI LA database as an active licensee, proving authenticity to customs officials, distributors, and consumers.
  • Testing and Certification Privileges: Only active Adopters can submit products to HDMI Authorized Test Centers (ATCs) for compliance testing.

Additional Costs to Consider

The annual fee is separate from other mandatory costs associated with HDMI integration:

  • Per-Unit Royalties: Active Adopters must pay a royalty on every licensed end-user product sold. The standard royalty rate is $0.15 per unit, which can be discounted to $0.05 if the HDMI logo is properly displayed, or $0.04 if High-Bandwidth Digital Content Protection (HDCP) is also implemented.
  • Compliance Testing Fees: Manufacturers must cover the testing fees charged by independent Authorized Test Centers to certify their first product of each design category.
  • HDCP Licensing: If products use HDCP, a separate annual fee and royalty must be paid to Digital Content Protection (DCP) LLC.

Consequences of Non-Payment

If a manufacturer fails to pay the annual $10,000 fee, their HDMI Adopter Agreement is subject to termination, placing their account into inactive status. Inactive status results in the immediate revocation of the right to use HDMI intellectual property. Any continued manufacturing, distribution, or marketing of HDMI-enabled products without active status constitutes trademark and patent infringement, potentially leading to product seizures by international customs authorities and legal action.
