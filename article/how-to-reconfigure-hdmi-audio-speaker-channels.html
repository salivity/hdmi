How to Reconfigure HDMI Audio Speaker Channels

Connecting a computer to a home theater AV receiver via HDMI often results in mismatched or incorrectly routed surround channels, such as side surrounds playing through rear speakers or an inverted center channel. This guide explains how to remap and correct multi-channel HDMI speaker layouts using native operating system settings, dedicated audio digital signal processing (DSP) tools, and media player channel-mapping features.

Using Native Windows Sound Settings

Windows includes a built-in configuration utility to establish standard speaker layouts (5.1, 7.1, or Atmos):

  1. Press Windows Key + R, type mmsys.cpl, and press Enter to open the classic Sound Control Panel.
  2. Under the Playback tab, locate and click your HDMI output (typically labeled with your receiver's brand or TV model).
  3. Click the Configure button in the lower-left corner.
  4. Select your audio configuration (e.g., 5.1 Surround or 7.1 Surround) and click Test to send test tones to each speaker.
  5. If the layout allows, check or uncheck optional speakers (such as surround speakers versus rear speakers) to match your physical setup. Click Next until the wizard finishes, then click Finish.

Advanced Software Remapping with Equalizer APO (Windows)

If Windows sends channels to the wrong physical speakers and native settings cannot fix the routing, use Equalizer APO, an open-source parametric equalizer and audio routing engine.

  1. Download and install Equalizer APO.
  2. During setup, the Configurator utility will open. Select your HDMI audio endpoint under the Playback devices tab and complete the installation (a system restart is required).
  3. Open the Configuration Editor from the Equalizer APO start menu folder.
  4. Remove the default example filters by clicking the red minus (-) buttons.
  5. Click the green plus (+) icon, navigate to Basic filters, and select Copy (Channel matrix).
  6. A channel assignment grid will appear. Use the grid to route input channels to the desired physical output channels. For example, to swap the Center (C) and Subwoofer (LFE) channels, link the input C to output LFE, and input LFE to output C.
  7. Save the configuration. Equalizer APO applies the channel reassignments instantly in real time at the system level.

Reconfiguring Audio Channels on macOS

macOS manages multi-channel HDMI routing through its native MIDI manager:

  1. Open Finder, navigate to Applications > Utilities, and open Audio MIDI Setup.
  2. Select your HDMI or AV Receiver device from the left sidebar.
  3. In the lower-right area of the window, click Configure Speakers....
  4. Select your setup from the configuration drop-down menu (such as 5.1 Surround).
  5. Click on individual speaker icons to test playback.
  6. To reassign a channel, click the drop-down menu beneath any speaker label and manually assign it to a different stream number from the HDMI output.
  7. Click Apply and then Done.

Media Player-Level Channel Mapping

If channel misalignment only occurs during media playback, you can correct it directly inside media software without altering system-wide settings:

  • VLC Media Player: Navigate to Tools > Preferences, select All under Show settings, go to Audio > Filters, and enable audio channel mapping or remixing modules.
  • Kodi: Go to Settings > System > Audio. Set the number of channels to match your receiver, select WASAPI (on Windows) or the direct HDMI hardware output, and adjust stereo upmixing or channel mapping flags.
  • MPC-HC / MPC-BE: Open Options > Internal Filters > Audio Switcher. Check Enable custom channel mapping to expose a complete grid where input channels can be freely assigned to any output speaker.
