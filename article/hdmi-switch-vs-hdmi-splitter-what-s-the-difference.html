HDMI Switch vs. HDMI Splitter: What's the Difference?

While an HDMI switch and an HDMI splitter look similar, they serve completely opposite purposes in an audio-video setup. An HDMI switch allows you to connect multiple source devices to a single display, letting you toggle between them. Conversely, an HDMI splitter takes a single video signal from one device and duplicates it across multiple screens simultaneously. Understanding the direction in which your video signal needs to travel is the key to choosing the correct device for your needs.

What is an HDMI Switch?

An HDMI switch takes multiple inputs and sends them to a single output.

  • How it works: Many inputs \(\rightarrow\) One output.
  • Common scenario: Your television has only two HDMI ports, but you own four devices: a gaming console, a streaming stick, a Blu-ray player, and a cable box.
  • Functionality: You plug all your source devices into the switch, and run a single HDMI cable from the switch to your TV. You then use a button on the unit or an included remote control to toggle between which device appears on the screen. Only one source is displayed at any given time.

What is an HDMI Splitter?

An HDMI splitter takes a single input and duplicates it across multiple outputs.

  • How it works: One input \(\rightarrow\) Many outputs.
  • Common scenario: You have one cable box or media player and want to show the exact same video feed on a TV in your living room and a TV in your kitchen at the same time.
  • Functionality: You plug your media device into the splitter's input port, and run separate HDMI cables from the splitter's output ports to each display. The splitter duplicates the audio and video signal so that both screens show identical content simultaneously.

Note on Splitters: HDMI splitters mirror screens; they do not extend your desktop across multiple monitors like a computer docking station does. Additionally, splitters typically default to the lowest resolution among the connected displays to maintain compatibility.

Summary Comparison

Feature HDMI Switch HDMI Splitter
Input / Output Multiple Inputs \(\rightarrow\) Single Output Single Input \(\rightarrow\) Multiple Outputs
Primary Purpose Connect multiple devices to one TV Mirror one device onto multiple TVs
Active Displays One screen displaying one source Multiple screens displaying identical content
Selection Method Manual toggle button or remote control Automatic, continuous signal mirroring

How to Choose

  • Buy an HDMI switch if you are constantly unplugging and swapping HDMI cables behind your TV because you have run out of available ports.
  • Buy an HDMI splitter if you want to broadcast the same media from a single console, PC, or media player to two or more screens at once.
