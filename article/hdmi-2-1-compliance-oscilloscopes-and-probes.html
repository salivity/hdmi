HDMI 2.1 Compliance Oscilloscopes and Probes

Testing HDMI 2.1 devices requires high-performance test equipment capable of validating Fixed Rate Link (FRL) architecture, which transfers data at speeds up to 12 Gbps per lane across four lanes (48 Gbps total). HDMI Forum Authorized Test Centers (ATCs) and compliance test specifications (CTS) mandate the use of high-bandwidth real-time oscilloscopes (minimum 16 GHz to 23+ GHz), automated compliance software, and precision test point access (TPA) fixtures. This guide covers the certified oscilloscope platforms from Keysight, Tektronix, and Teledyne LeCroy, along with the authorized probe fixtures required for HDMI 2.1 transmitter (Source) and receiver (Sink) physical-layer compliance testing.

Bandwidth and Hardware Requirements

HDMI 2.1 introduces FRL alongside legacy Transition Minimized Differential Signaling (TMDS). To accurately capture the fundamental and third-to-fifth harmonics of 12 Gbps signals without significant attenuation, the HDMI 2.1 Compliance Test Specification dictates:

  • Bandwidth: Minimum 16 GHz real-time bandwidth (recommended 20 GHz to 25 GHz to capture rise-time details and higher harmonics).
  • Sample Rate: Minimum 50 GS/s per channel across four simultaneous channels.
  • Channels: 4 synchronized channels (to capture all data lanes simultaneously).

Certified Real-Time Oscilloscope Platforms

The HDMI Forum validates automated compliance packages on specific high-frequency oscilloscope architectures:

1. Keysight Technologies

  • Oscilloscopes:
    • Infiniium UXR-Series: Specifically UXR0164A (16 GHz), UXR0254A (25 GHz), and higher models. The UXR provides native 10-bit vertical resolution and low noise floor.
    • Infiniium V-Series and Z-Series: Legacy configurations using DSA-V or DSA-Z models rated at 16 GHz to 25 GHz (e.g., MSOS164A, DSOZ204A).
  • Software: Keysight N6472A / N6472B HDMI 2.1 Compliance Test Software provides automated FRL electrical test execution, jitter analysis, and eye-diagram validation.

2. Tektronix

  • Oscilloscopes:
    • DPO70000SX / MSO70000SX Series: Certified configurations include 16 GHz, 20 GHz, and 23 GHz models (e.g., DPO71604SX, DPO72004SX, DPO72304SX). Multi-unit sync systems allow synchronized 4-channel acquisition at high bandwidth.
    • DPO/DSA72004C/D Series: 20 GHz real-time oscilloscopes supporting automated testing.
  • Software: TekExpress HDMI 2.1 Transmitter (TX) Solution (Option HDM21) automating electrical Source measurements, FRL link training verification, and pass/fail reporting.

3. Teledyne LeCroy

  • Oscilloscopes:
    • LabMaster 10 Zi-A / SDA 8 Zi-B Series: Multi-channel modular systems with bandwidths ranging from 16 GHz to 25 GHz.
    • WaveMaster 8000HD: 12-bit High Definition Oscilloscope configurations supporting up to 25 GHz bandwidth for multi-lane testing.
  • Software: QualiPHY HDMI 2.1 (QPHY-HDMI2.1-TX-RX) automated compliance test package.

Certified Test Fixtures and Probes

HDMI 2.1 physical-layer electrical compliance prohibits generic high-impedance probing directly on the PCB for official certification; testing must be performed at standard connector test points using certified Test Point Adapters (TPAs).

1. Wilder Technologies HDMI 2.1 Fixture Sets

Wilder Technologies manufactures the officially endorsed and standardized Category 3 TPA fixtures used by ATCs worldwide:

  • Plug Fixture (TF-HDMI-2.1-TPA-P): Connects to HDMI 2.1 Sink devices to verify input compliance and signal integrity.
  • Receptacle Fixture (TF-HDMI-2.1-TPA-R): Connects to HDMI 2.1 Source devices to measure output eye diagrams, jitter, differential voltage, and rise/fall times.
  • These fixtures break out high-speed FRL differential lanes into high-frequency precision RF connectors (typically 2.92mm / SMA) rated well above 20 GHz.

2. Signal Access and Probing Setup

  • Direct RF Cabling: High-speed differential pairs (FRL lanes 0–3) are connected directly from the Wilder TPA fixtures to the oscilloscope channels via matched-pair, phase-matched SMA/2.92mm coaxial cables to minimize channel-to-channel skew.
  • DC Termination and Bias: HDMI 2.1 Source testing requires pull-up terminations. Oscilloscopes require internal termination or external bias tees/termination networks (such as Keysight N1080B or vendor-specific HDMI termination fixtures) supplying a 3.3V pull-up to properly bias the differential current-mode outputs.
  • Active Differential Probes (Supplemental/Debug):
    • Keysight: InfiniiMax III/III+ Series (e.g., N2801A 20 GHz or N2802A 25 GHz) with solder-in probe heads for in-circuit verification.
    • Tektronix: P7700 or P7600 Series TriMode probes (e.g., P7720 20 GHz) for embedded troubleshooting.
    • Teledyne LeCroy: DH Series differential probes (e.g., DH20-PL 20 GHz).
