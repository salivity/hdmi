4K 60Hz HDMI Over Multi-Mode Fiber Distance

An uncompressed 4K at 60Hz HDMI signal can typically travel up to 300 meters (approximately 1,000 feet) using a multi-mode fiber optic converter paired with OM3 cabling. With higher-grade OM4 or OM5 multi-mode fiber, this distance can occasionally extend up to 400 or 500 meters depending on the specific converter hardware. This article explains the technical limits, cable types, and performance expectations when transmitting uncompressed 4K60 video over multi-mode optical systems.

Bandwidth and Transmission Limits

Transmitting uncompressed 4K video at 60Hz with 4:4:4 chroma subsampling requires an HDMI 2.0 bandwidth of roughly 18 Gbps. Because copper HDMI cables degrade rapidly beyond 5 to 10 meters at these speeds, optical extenders convert the electrical HDMI signal into optical light pulses.

Multi-mode fiber optic converters use vertical-cavity surface-emitting lasers (VCSELs) operating at an 850 nm wavelength. The maximum range of an uncompressed signal depends heavily on the modal dispersion of the fiber core and the optical bandwidth classification of the cable:

  • OM1 / OM2 (Legacy): Not recommended for 18 Gbps signals. Distances are typically limited to under 30 meters.
  • OM3 (Laser-Optimized): The industry standard for most AV installations. Supports uncompressed 4K@60Hz up to 300 meters (1,000 feet).
  • OM4 / OM5: Features higher modal bandwidth. Extends uncompressed 4K@60Hz signals up to 400 to 500 meters (1,300 to 1,640 feet), provided the converter optics support the extended link budget.

Why Multi-Mode Distance Tops Out at 300–400 Meters

Multi-mode fiber uses a wider core diameter (typically 50 microns for OM3 and OM4) compared to single-mode fiber (9 microns). This wider diameter allows multiple modes of light to travel down the core simultaneously. Over longer distances, these light modes travel slightly different paths and arrive at different times—a phenomenon known as modal dispersion. At data rates as high as 18 Gbps, this dispersion causes signal degradation, effectively capping the reliable distance of uncompressed video to around 300 meters on standard OM3.

Multi-Mode vs. Single-Mode for 4K60

If an installation requires distances beyond 300 to 400 meters, single-mode fiber converters are necessary. Single-mode systems allow only a single path of light to propagate, eliminating modal dispersion and enabling uncompressed 4K@60Hz transmission across distances ranging from 10 kilometers to 40 kilometers. However, for internal enterprise, campus, and broadcast studio setups within a 300-meter footprint, multi-mode fiber remains the standard and most cost-effective solution.
